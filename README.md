## What this is

ICH E6(R3) requires that clinical trial staff be "qualified by education,
training and experience." It does not say what qualified means in a way anyone
can measure, so the industry answers with completion certificates that record
attendance.

CRLN-TRG-001 is an attempt at the missing half: a role spine, ten canonical
competency domains, weighted competency statements, a four-level scale, scoring
rules, and a crosswalk to ICH E6(R3) and the JTF Clinical Trial Competency
Framework.

It is a **specification, not a product**. It contains no personal data, has no
users, hosts no content and enables no interaction between people. Anyone can
implement it, including in competition with CRLN.

## Translations

Translations are provided so that an organisation can read the framework in a
language it works in. They are **unofficial**. Where a translation and the
English differ, the English governs, and that is stated at the top of each
translated file.

This follows the practice of ICH itself and exists for a specific reason: a
mistranslated competency statement in a framework somebody mandates is a real
problem, not a cosmetic one. Corrections from native speakers are welcome and are
the fastest way to improve them.

## The conflict of interest, stated plainly

CRLN publishes this framework openly and also operates a commercial platform that
implements it. **CRLN therefore has a direct commercial interest in the
framework's adoption.** That cannot be removed while both activities sit in one
organisation; it can only be disclosed and constrained. `GOVERNANCE.md` sets out
the constraints, including a Methodology Review Board with a majority
unaffiliated with CRLN, which is not yet constituted.

The framework and crosswalk are openly licensed. The measurement engine, item
parameters and learner data are proprietary and are **not** licensed here. A
change to the framework that advantaged CRLN's implementation over anyone else's
would be a governance failure, and it is the specific risk the Board exists to
check.

## Proposing a change

Anyone may. No account, affiliation or fee required. Open an issue here or email
`standard@crln-learn.com`. See `GOVERNANCE.md` §4 for what happens next and how
long it takes.
