## The source, and why it matters

**TDR Global Competency Framework for Clinical Research** (2016, ISBN 978 92 4 151155 1), published by the UNICEF/UNDP/World Bank/WHO Special Programme for Research and Training in Tropical Diseases (TDR), with The Global Health Network.
50 competencies. <https://globalhealthtrainingcentre.tghn.org/pds/core-competency-framework/>

It has been the reference for competency in clinical research capacity strengthening in
low- and middle-income settings since 2016. A competency framework published in 2026 that
ignores it is incomplete. CRLN-TRG-001 ignored it until this document existed.

**Licence note.** TDR's publication states that use of its content for non-commercial
education, training and information purposes is encouraged, including translation,
quotation and reproduction in any medium, provided the content is **not changed** and the
source is fully acknowledged. TDR competency names below are reproduced verbatim and
unaltered on that basis. This crosswalk does not modify, replace or extend TDR's framework.

## The structural difference, which explains most of the result

**TDR describes what a research *team* must collectively be able to do, across the whole
research enterprise.** Designing the science, winning the funding, engaging the community,
managing the people, analysing and publishing the results.

**CRLN-TRG-001 describes what an *individual in a named role* must be able to do during
trial execution, and grades them on it.** It begins after a protocol exists and ends when
the database locks.

These are different units of analysis. They are complementary, not competing, and the
numbers below are a measure of that difference rather than a score either way.

## What TDR has that CRLN does not

This is the useful half of the document. Each of these is a gap in CRLN-TRG-001.

**1. Health-related knowledge** · *Theory (knowledge-based)*  
CRLN assumes health-related background as a precondition and never assesses it. TDR treats it as a competency in its own right.

**2. Research methodology** · *Theory (knowledge-based)*  
Research methodology as a knowledge domain is absent from CRLN, which begins after a protocol exists.

**3. Developing a protocol** · *Task-based*  
Protocol authorship. CRLN is execution-side only and assesses adherence to a protocol, never its design.

**4. Attracting funding** · *Task-based*  
Attracting funding has no CRLN counterpart at any level.

**7. Analysing data** · *Task-based*  
Statistical analysis is absent from CRLN. CDM covers data management and lock, not analysis.

**8. Disseminating research findings** · *Task-based*  
Dissemination and publication have no CRLN counterpart.

**11. Determining liability and insurance needs** · *Task-based*  
Liability and insurance are absent from CRLN.

**16. Securing or maintaining contracts** · *Task-based*  
Contracts and budgets are absent from CRLN, which does not assess commercial or legal negotiation.

**25. Facilitating or attending meetings** · *Task-based*  
Meeting facilitation is not assessed by CRLN.

**26. Human resources** · *Task-based*  
Human resources management has no CRLN counterpart.

**27. Creating or delivering training** · *Task-based*  
Creating or delivering training is absent from CRLN, which is itself a training provider but does not assess the competency.

**28. Supervising or mentoring** · *Task-based*  
Supervision and mentoring are absent from CRLN.

**39. Engaging with the community** · *Task-based*  
COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT IS ABSENT FROM CRLN. This is the most consequential gap, and it is precisely the competency TDR added for low-resource and community-based research settings.

**42. Cognitive skills** · *Skill-bases*  
CRLN does not assess general cognitive skills.

**43. Strategic leadership** · *Skill-based*  
Strategic leadership is absent from CRLN.

**44. Interpersonal skills** · *Skill-based*  
Interpersonal skills are not assessed as a competency.

**45. Language and communication skills** · *Skill-based*  
Language and communication skills are not assessed, although CRLN renders in 60 languages.

**46. Organisational skills** · *Skill-based*  
Organisational skills are not assessed as a competency.

**48. Computer and information & technology (IT) skills** · *Skill-based*  
General IT skills are not assessed.

**49. Work ethic** · *Skill-based*  
Work ethic is not assessed.

The single most consequential is **Engaging with the community**. It is the competency TDR
added for low-resource and community-based research, it is absent from CRLN entirely, and
its absence is exactly the kind of blind spot a framework written from a US and European
starting point would have.
