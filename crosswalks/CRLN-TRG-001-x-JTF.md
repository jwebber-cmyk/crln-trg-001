## Why this document exists, stated plainly

The mapping below has existed in CRLN's review register since the review, and every release
since has *described* it in prose. **It was never published as a table anyone could check.**
A framework that says it is crosswalked domain by domain, without showing the domains, is
asking to be taken on trust. This removes that.

Source for the JTF domain list: <https://mrctcenter.org/clinical-trial-competency/framework/domains/>

## What CRLN does NOT map to, which is the more useful half

The JTF Framework has eight domains. CRLN's ten canonical domains map onto **five** of them.

| JTF domain | CRLN coverage |
|---:|---|
| 1. Scientific Concepts and Research Design | **no CRLN counterpart** |
| 2. Ethical and Participant Safety Considerations | mapped |
| 3. Investigational Products Development and Regulation | mapped |
| 4. Clinical Study Operations (GCPs) | mapped |
| 5. Study and Site Management | mapped |
| 6. Data Management and Informatics | mapped |
| 7. Leadership and Professionalism | **one secondary mapping only**, no canonical domain |
| 8. Communication and Teamwork | **no CRLN counterpart** |

**Domain 1, Scientific Concepts and Research Design, and Domain 8, Communication and
Teamwork, have no CRLN counterpart at all. Domain 7, Leadership and Professionalism, has
one secondary mapping and no canonical domain.**

This is not an oversight and it is not a claim that those domains matter less. CRLN-TRG-001
assesses what an individual in a named role does during trial execution, beginning after a
protocol exists. Designing the science, leading a team and communicating within one are
real competencies that this instrument does not measure.

**Three independent crosswalks have now found the same shape.** The TDR Global Competency
Framework crosswalk found research methodology, protocol development, strategic leadership
and interpersonal skills unmapped. The EU CTR 536/2014 crosswalk found the framework silent
on trial design. And JTF domains 1, 7 and 8 are the same absence seen from a third angle.
That convergence is worth more than any single mapping row below it.

## A caveat a reviewer should know before reading further

CRLN holds a second, finer-grained JTF mapping at the level of individual competency
statements, with primary and secondary designations. **That finer mapping is
`provisional_unreviewed` and is NOT covered by the validation above.** Only the
ten-canonical-domain table in this document was reviewed. Anyone citing CRLN's JTF
alignment should cite this table and not the statement-level mapping.

## What would be most useful from the JTF

A correction, in preference to agreement. Specifically:

1. Is any row in the table above wrong? `D-01 GCP Principles & Ethics` mapping to JTF 4
   rather than JTF 2 is the row the custodian is least sure of.
2. Is mapping three CRLN domains (`D-02`, `D-04`, `D-07`) all to JTF 2 a reasonable
   reading, or does it flatten distinctions the JTF framework draws deliberately?
3. Does the JTF consider domains 1, 7 and 8 essential to any competency claim, such that
   an instrument omitting them should say so more prominently than this document does?
