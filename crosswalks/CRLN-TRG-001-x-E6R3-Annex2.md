## Why this exists now

**ICH E6(R3) Good Clinical Practice, Annex 2** was adopted on **2026-06-03** and comes into effect on **2027-01-15**.
Covers decentralised elements, pragmatic elements and the use of real-world data.

CRLN-TRG-001 has a Decentralized Trial Coordinator track whose statements cite
"ICH E6(R3) Annex 2" as a whole, with no section. They cite it that way because no adopted
Annex 2 existed when they were written. One now does, and this document replaces a gesture
with a citation.

Source: <https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/scientific-guideline/ich-e6r3-guideline-good-clinical-practice-annex-2-step-5_en.pdf>

## The finding: real-world data is absent from CRLN

**Four of the five gaps are the same gap.** Annex 2 devotes §3.4, §3.4.1, §3.4.2 and §3.5.1
to real-world data: obtaining access to it, consent and permission for its secondary use,
and its provenance, quality and fitness for purpose.

**CRLN-TRG-001 does not mention real-world data anywhere.** Every consent statement in the
framework is trial-participant consent. Consent and permission for secondary use of existing
health data is a materially different problem, and the framework is silent on it.

This becomes a regulatory expectation on **2027-01-15**.

The fifth gap, §3.2 Protocol and trial design, is out of scope by construction rather than
by oversight: CRLN begins after a protocol exists.

## Notes per section

**§2.1 Communication with IRB/IEC** · full  
Maps to the PI and RA tracks. Annex 2 adds decentralised-specific expectations that CRLN does not yet distinguish from conventional IRB communication.

**§2.2 Informed consent considerations** · full  
Strong. CRLN-DCT-D2 was written for exactly this and can now cite a section rather than the Annex generally.

**§2.3 Investigational product management** · full  
Strong, via the DCT and PHARM tracks.

**§2.4 Investigator oversight** · full  
Maps to the PI track and the delegation statements.

**§2.5 Safety assessment and reporting** · full  
Strong, via DCT-D4 and the safety statements across tracks.

**§3.1 Engagement and communication** · partial  
CRLN covers sponsor-site communication but not participant and community engagement in trial design, which is what Annex 2 emphasises.

**§3.2 Protocol and trial design** · none  
CRLN is execution-side and begins after a protocol exists. Trial design is out of scope by construction, not by oversight.

**§3.3 Communication with IRB/IEC** · full  
Maps to the RA and CTA tracks.

**§3.4 Access to and use of RWD** · none  
NO CRLN COUNTERPART. Real-world data does not appear anywhere in CRLN-TRG-001.

**§3.4.1 Access to RWD** · none  
NO CRLN COUNTERPART. Nothing in the framework addresses obtaining or governing access to real-world data sources.

**§3.4.2 Consent and permission considerations for RWD** · none  
NO CRLN COUNTERPART. CRLN's consent statements are all trial-participant consent. Consent and permission for secondary use of real-world data is a different problem and is absent.

**§3.5 Data considerations** · partial  
CRLN covers trial data integrity well; the Annex 2 additions concern data not generated by the trial.

**§3.5.1 RWD Considerations** · none  
NO CRLN COUNTERPART. Provenance, fitness for purpose and quality of real-world data are unaddressed.

**§3.5.2 Remote data collection considerations** · full  
Strong. DCT-D3 and DCT-D6 were written for this and can now cite the section.

**§3.6 Investigational product management** · full  
Strong, across DCT, PHARM and CRA.

**§3.7 Privacy and confidentiality considerations** · partial  
Only one CRLN statement, on the ASSIST track, addresses confidentiality. Annex 2 treats privacy as a sponsor-level design obligation, which CRLN does not cover.

**§3.8 Sponsor oversight** · full  
Strong, across DCT, PM and CRA.

**§3.9 Safety assessment and reporting** · full  
Strong, via the PV track.

## What should change in CRLN-TRG-001

Recorded as a consequence rather than a plan, because the Methodology Review Board is below
quorum and cannot approve a change of this size:

1. The DCT statements citing "Annex 2" generally should cite sections.
2. Real-world data has no home in the framework. Adding it is a MINOR version change at
   least, because it adds competencies rather than reworking existing ones.
3. Whether RWD belongs in the CDM track, the DCT track, or a new one is a question for
   reviewers rather than for the custodian.
